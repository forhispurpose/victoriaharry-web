About Victoria Harry Interiors

Design that starts with how you want to live.

Meet the Designer

Victoria Harry

Victoria Harry is an interior designer dedicated to creating spaces that feel authentic, balanced, and timeless. Combining functional spatial planning with bespoke textures and warm aesthetics, she transforms houses into homes that truly inspire.

With an eye for subtle luxury and thoughtful detail, Victoria curates environments where every piece serves a purpose and every corner feels welcoming. Whether overseeing a full residential transformation or styling a single room, she partners closely with each client to bring their personal vision to life.
